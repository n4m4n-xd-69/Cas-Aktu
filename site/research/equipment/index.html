    Major equipment

    48 labs and instruments migrated from the legacy site's equipment list.

    • Nvidia DGX-2 Server (two petaFlops system)
      AI Lab GPU server — NVIDIA DGX-2, described in the source as the world's first 2-petaflops system, packing 16 GPUs.
    • BET Measurement System
      Determines the surface area of materials.
    • Industrial Robotic Center
      KUKA India-built center with three robotic cells: material handling, MIG welding, and bare robotic.
    • Industrial Automation Lab
      Flexible Manufacturing System: AS/RS storage, CNC lathe/milling/grinding, 4- and 6-axis robots, AGV, assembly station.
    • 3D Printing Lab
      Additive manufacturing; two printers using fused material deposition and filament-based polymer deposition.
    • Design and Simulation Center
      18 high-end workstations running SolidWorks, ANSYS, and MATLAB; integrated with the 3D Printing Lab.
    • Sensor Drives & Control Lab
      Teaches sensor/controller/drive fundamentals and microcontroller programming in C++, Python, and MATLAB.
    • Pin on Disc Tribometer
      Ducom Pin/Ball on Disk Tribometer for tribological characterization of materials, coatings, and lubricants.
    • Human Non-invasive Blood Pressure (NIBP) Monitor
      Finger arterial pressure, systolic/diastolic, and heart-rate monitoring with Volume Clamp Technology.
    • Equi-vital Physiological Monitoring System
      Wearable belt recording ECG, 3-axis acceleration, respiration, skin temperature, SpO2, and GSR.
    • Markerless Motion Capture System (2-D Gait Analysis) with Force Plate
      16x2 ft platform measuring gait parameters — velocity, cadence, step length, centre of pressure.
    • EMOTIV EPOC+ 14-Channel Mobile EEG
      Wireless EEG and 9-axis motion data over Bluetooth for brain-computer interface research.
    • Auditory Brainstem Response (ABR) Audiometry System
      Neurologic test of auditory brainstem function via evoked potentials from click/tone stimuli.
    • Delsys Wireless EMG System
      Wireless sensors recording electrical muscle activity for exercise physiology and rehabilitation research.
    • FLIR E5 Thermal Imaging Camera
      10,800-pixel (120x90) infrared resolution, focus-free thermal imaging camera.
    • SENSEnuts
      IoT prototyping stack — microcontroller with 802.15.4 transceiver plus environmental and meteorological sensors.
    • Material Chemistry & Synthesis Laboratory
      Fume hood, spot extractors, magnetic stirrers, ultrasonic processor, vacuum furnace for nanomaterial synthesis.
    • Field Emission Scanning Electron Microscope (FE-SEM) with EDAX Module
      Views shape, size, texture, and elemental composition of materials at micro/nano scale.
    • Electrochemical Workstation
      Electrochemical energy storage, heavy-metal ion detection, and catalysis research.
    • Impedance Analyser
      Electrical impedance characterization for fuel cells, photovoltaic cells, and batteries.
    • Voyager Cyber City Simulator
      Automated modeling and monitoring of cyber-security threats across a simulated smart-city environment.
    • Solar Simulator
      Simulates natural sunlight in lab conditions for testing solar cells and modules.
    • HP Z238 Microtower Workstations, Google Developers Code Lab
      Collaboration with Google Asia Pacific Pvt. Ltd.; replicates a Google-style coding environment for students.
    • Augmented Reality Lab
      HP Z2 G5 workstation and HPE ProLiant ML350 Gen10 server running AR Vuforia Studio, CREO Illustrate, and ThingWorx.
    • Microwave Reactor (Anton Paar Monowave 450)
      High-performance monomode microwave reactor for synthesis up to 300°C and 30 bar.
    • Drop Shape Analyzer — DSA100
      Measures contact angle and surface free energy for wetting/adhesion analysis on solid surfaces.
    • Automatic Micro Hardness Testing System
      Vickers hardness testing via controlled diamond indentation.
    • Atomic Force Microscopy NX10
      Park NX10 AFM for nanoscale materials research, including True Non-Contact mode.
    • FTIR Spectrophotometer Alpha-II
      Identifies compounds (plastics, blends, coatings, resins, adhesives) via infrared spectroscopy.
    • UV-Vis Spectrophotometer 3092
      Qualitative and quantitative molecular analysis via UV-visible absorbance spectroscopy.
    • Drone Systems Lab
      Three drone systems (professional-grade, mini, and multispectral-sensor) for aerial imaging research.
    • Thermogravimetric Analyser (TGA)
      Monitors sample mass versus temperature/time up to 1600°C for material characterization.
    • Spray Pyrolysis Unit
      PC-automated thin-film deposition system, used especially for solar cell development.
    • Nano Fiber Electrospinning
      Produces nano/micro fibers (50nm-5 microns) from polymers, carbon nanotubes, and other materials.
    • Four-Probe Workstation
      Measures sheet resistance and resistivity of conducting and semiconducting materials.
    • Dip Coating Unit
      Programmable substrate dip-coating with heating-chamber temperature control up to 75°C.
    • Spin Coater
      Tabletop spin-coating system with programmable spin duration, speed, and acceleration.
    • Lee's Disc Thermal Conductivity Apparatus
      Measures thermal conductivity in poor conductors via a brass-disc heat-flow setup.
    • Hall Effect Measurement
      Holmarc's apparatus measuring Hall voltage as a function of current, magnetic flux, and temperature using doped germanium samples.
    • Stereo Zoom Microscope
      Stereo imaging with 0.63x-6.3x zoom (3.15x-378x effective magnification).
    • Quantum Efficiency System
      Measures spectral response and quantum efficiency of solar cells across 400-1200nm.
    • Micro EDM Machine
      Fabricates 3D micro-scale structures for MEMS and biomedical-implant applications.
    • Optical Microscope
      Metallurgical microscope for microstructure, grain boundary, and porosity analysis.
    • Die Electric-Discharge Machining (with MQL System)
      Non-traditional machining via repeated electrical discharges, for hard-to-machine components.
    • PLC SCADA — Part A
      Description pending — see note on this record
    • Language Lab with Headphones and i7 Systems
      Dell Optiplex i7 systems with headphones and audio-visual software for language learning.
    • Linux / Open Source Lab
      22 Dell Optiplex i7 systems running Ubuntu Linux and Kali Linux.
    • General Purpose Computing Lab
      32 Dell Optiplex systems on Windows 10 with development tooling (Java SDK, GNS3, Android SDK, Visual Studio, and others).
