    About the institute

    An in-campus research institute of AKTU

    Established by the Uttar Pradesh State Government in 2017, the Centre for Advanced Studies offers M.Tech. and Ph.D. programmes in computer science and engineering, mechatronics, nanotechnology, manufacturing technology and automation, and energy science and technology, alongside B.Tech. offerings.

    Its stated objective is to give research scholars and academicians a platform to create and disseminate research-based knowledge and technologies.

    More about CAS

    Disciplines

    Five departments, one interdisciplinary campus

    Each department runs its own laboratories, doctoral programme and funded research portfolio — and collaborates across the others.

    Campus life

    A campus designed for focused work

    Purpose-built laboratories, high-speed connectivity, on-campus residence and a digital library — everything within a single secure campus.

    Campus-wide connectivity
    1 Gbps Wi-Fi with 1 Gbps LAN connectivity and 10 Gbps traffic support across the campus.
    24-hour security
    HD CCTV surveillance, round-the-clock security at entry points, and a full perimeter compound wall.
    Residential facilities
    Separate hostels for men and women, approximately 75 single-seater rooms each, with internet and security.
    Digital library
    Access to international science and engineering books, journals, and e-resource consortia.
